# Cute clownfish style study

These concept assets were generated with the built-in ImageGen tool. Every fish uses the same production constraints: one right-facing ocellaris clownfish, full body, compact rounded silhouette, friendly expressive eye, tiny natural fish mouth, exactly three white bands with black edging, transparent alpha background, no scenery, no text, and no extra fish.

## A - Cute animation 3D

Polished family-animation 3D rendering, soft underwater key light, rounded compact anatomy, glossy expressive eye, friendly but not babyish, crisp at small mobile size.

## B - Cute storybook gouache

Warm storybook gouache and digital hand-painting, tactile brush texture, subtle color variation, cozy and refined, clear at small mobile size.

## C - Cute cel-shaded mascot

High-end 2D cel shading, clean dark outlines, simple two-step highlights and shadows, modern game-character clarity, cute without extreme chibi proportions.

## D - Cute soft-clay 3D

Premium soft-clay stop-motion rendering, smooth handcrafted surface, warm glossy eye, tactile and refined rather than toy-like.

## Aquarium comparison composite

Preserve the supplied aquarium background unchanged. Insert one copy of A, B, C, and D in a separated 2-by-2 layout, all facing right and at equal apparent size. Add small A-D badges and only subtle underwater light integration. Do not redesign the fish or add other content.
